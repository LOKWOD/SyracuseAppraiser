Independent residential valuation

A divorce-related appraisal answers an identified valuation question without acting as an advocate for either party. Depending on the assignment, the appraisal may require a current value or a retrospective value tied to a prior date.

What we typically need

  • property address
  • requested effective date or dates
  • intended use and intended users
  • access arrangements
  • assignment-specific requirements supplied by the client

Clear market support

The appraisal provides an independent opinion of value supported by relevant market evidence and an explanation of the subject property, comparable sales and reconciliation.

Scope boundary

The appraisal provides valuation services. Legal questions should be addressed by the appropriate professional adviser.

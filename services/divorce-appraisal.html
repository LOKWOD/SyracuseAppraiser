A valuation opinion—not advocacy for either side

A divorce-related appraisal should answer a valuation question without acting as an advocate for either spouse. Depending on counsel’s needs, the assignment may require a current value, a retrospective value tied to a prior date, or more than one effective date.

What we typically need

  • property address
  • requested effective date or dates
  • intended use and intended users
  • access arrangements
  • any assignment-specific requirements supplied by counsel

Clear market support

The appraisal provides an independent opinion of value supported by market evidence and a clear explanation of the subject property, comparable sales and reconciliation.

Scope boundary

We do not provide legal opinions regarding marital versus separate property, equitable distribution, ownership rights or how a court should use the appraisal.

Valuation as of the date that matters

Estate work often requires a credible opinion of value as of a specific date. That may be the present, the date of death or another historical effective date identified by the client and their professional adviser. The appraisal is developed around that effective date and intended use rather than simply reporting today’s value.

What we need to get started

  • property address
  • requested effective date
  • intended use of the appraisal
  • client and intended users
  • access contact and available property-condition information

What the report provides

The report explains the subject property, relevant market evidence, comparable sales, approaches to value considered and the reasoning supporting the final opinion. Retrospective assignments use market data and conditions appropriate to the historical effective date.

Important

We provide valuation services, not legal or tax advice. Attorneys, executors and tax professionals should determine the legal purpose, required effective date and filing requirements.

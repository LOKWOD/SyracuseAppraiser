Independent value before a major decision

A listing price is a marketing decision. An appraisal is an independent valuation assignment. For owners who want a separate, non-sales opinion of value, a private appraisal can establish a defensible benchmark before making a decision.

Common reasons to order

  • before listing a property
  • private or family sale
  • buying without conventional lender valuation
  • ownership transfer
  • financial planning or internal decision-making
  • complex or unique property where online estimates are unreliable

What the report does—and does not do

The appraisal analyzes the subject property and relevant market evidence to develop an opinion of value under the agreed scope. It does not guarantee a future sale price; actual sale price can be affected by exposure, negotiation, financing, concessions, timing and buyer motivation.

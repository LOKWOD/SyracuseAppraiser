The closest sale is not automatically the best sale

Waterfront, acreage, rural and custom properties often have fewer truly comparable transactions. Market competition and functional similarity matter more than an arbitrary radius.

Factors that can materially change value

  • water frontage, access, view and shoreline utility
  • usable acreage versus raw acreage
  • topography, access and privacy
  • well, septic and other private utilities
  • barns, detached garages and accessory improvements
  • custom construction quality and effective age
  • buyer pool and competing locations

Expanded comparable research

When the immediate neighborhood does not contain adequate substitutes, the analysis can expand geographically while explaining why the selected sales are relevant and how location differences are considered.

Central New York specialty

This work includes Skaneateles Lake, Oneida Lake and other water-influenced properties as well as rural acreage and custom homes throughout the region.

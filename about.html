ABOUT THE FIRM

Independent residential appraisal focused on Central New York.

Syracuse Appraiser is the local information site for Accurate Real Estate Appraisals, a division of ARE Appraisals, Inc.

Valuation, not a sales pitch.

An appraisal is an independent opinion of value developed for an identified use and effective date. Our work is built around market evidence and a defined scope of work.

Local context matters.

The Syracuse region ranges from city neighborhoods and suburbs to villages, lakefront property, farms, acreage and custom homes. Market boundaries are not always the same as ZIP codes or postal names.

Residential property types

  • single-family residences
  • small residential income properties where appropriate
  • vacant residential land
  • waterfront and water-influenced property
  • rural acreage and custom homes

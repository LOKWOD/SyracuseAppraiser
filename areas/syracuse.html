LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Appraisal work should reflect the market buyers actually use.

Syracuse is not one uniform housing market. Eastwood, Sedgwick, Strathmore, Tipp Hill, the Eastside, Northside, Southside and the university-area submarkets can differ materially in housing stock, condition, density, buyer expectations and typical price ranges. Comparable selection should reflect those distinctions rather than relying on the nearest sale.

Older housing stock, converted residences, small income properties and properties with atypical renovation or condition often require more analysis than a simple automated estimate can provide.

Property characteristics we consider

No single feature determines value. The appraisal considers the interaction of location, site, improvements and market evidence. Depending on the assignment, relevant factors may include:

  • neighborhood and micro-location
  • architectural style and age
  • condition and renovation quality
  • single-family versus 2–4 family use
  • lot utility, parking and garage availability
  • proximity to employment, universities and major corridors

Appraisals for private and professional use

We provide residential appraisal services for estate and date-of-death needs, divorce and equitable-distribution matters, pre-listing and private decisions, retrospective valuation, tax-assessment review, mortgage-related assignments and complex residential properties.

The appropriate scope of work depends on who needs the appraisal, why it is needed and the effective date of value.

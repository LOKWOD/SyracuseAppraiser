LOCAL VALUATION

One city. Many distinct housing markets.

Syracuse contains materially different residential submarkets. Eastwood, Strathmore, Sedgwick, Tipp Hill, the Eastside, Northside, Southside and other neighborhoods can differ in housing stock, buyer expectations, condition, density and typical price ranges. Comparable selection should reflect those market distinctions rather than treating every nearby sale as equally relevant.

We provide appraisal services for single-family homes, 2–4 family properties where appropriate, residential land and complex residential assignments throughout the city and surrounding suburbs.

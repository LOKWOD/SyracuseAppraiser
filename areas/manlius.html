LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Village, subdivision and rural markets require different comparable sets.

The Manlius market includes established village housing, suburban subdivisions, newer custom construction and lower-density acreage properties. School district, exact municipality, site characteristics and neighborhood appeal can all influence which sales are most relevant.

Larger custom homes, acreage and properties with substantial updates can require expanded comparable searches and careful adjustment support.

Property characteristics we consider

  • village versus town location
  • school district and neighborhood
  • site size and topography
  • custom features and quality
  • age and effective age
  • subdivision versus rural setting

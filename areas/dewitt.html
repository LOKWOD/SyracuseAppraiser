LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Municipal and school-district boundaries can change the competitive market.

DeWitt contains several distinct residential environments, from established suburban neighborhoods to higher-end pockets and areas influenced by different school districts and postal names. The correct market area is based on actual location and buyer behavior, not simply the mailing city.

Municipal and school-district boundaries can be especially important when selecting comparable sales in the eastern Syracuse suburbs.

Property characteristics we consider

  • actual municipality and school district
  • East Syracuse or Jamesville mailing address
  • neighborhood age and design
  • condition and renovation quality
  • lot utility and privacy
  • traffic and commercial influence

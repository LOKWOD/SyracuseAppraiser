LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Suburban and Oneida Lake markets require different comparable logic.

Cicero combines established suburban neighborhoods, newer development, rural-fringe properties and Oneida Lake waterfront or near-water locations. Waterfront properties require analysis of frontage, access, views, site utility and the relevant competitive lake market.

Waterfront and unusual acreage properties often have fewer close substitutes and may require geographically broader comparable research.

Property characteristics we consider

  • Oneida Lake frontage or proximity
  • subdivision and new-construction competition
  • school district
  • site size and utilities
  • road influence and accessibility
  • condition and effective age

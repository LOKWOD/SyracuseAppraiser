LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Lakefront, village and rural properties compete differently.

Skaneateles includes several distinct value influences: the village, lakefront and lake-view properties, rural acreage, newer custom homes and older housing stock. Lakefront valuation requires attention to frontage, access, view, site utility, improvements, shoreline characteristics and the competitive set of buyers.

Waterfront and high-end properties may have relatively few truly comparable sales. A supportable appraisal often requires a broader search, careful qualitative analysis and reconciliation of multiple market indicators.

Property characteristics we consider

  • lake frontage and access
  • water view and elevation
  • village versus rural location
  • site size and acreage
  • custom construction and renovation quality
  • seasonal versus year-round utility

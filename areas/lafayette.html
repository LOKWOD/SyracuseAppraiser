RURAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Distance alone is a poor measure of comparability in rural markets.

LaFayette is predominantly a lower-density rural market with acreage, custom homes, agricultural influence and properties served by private well and septic systems. Proximity alone is often a poor measure of comparability because site size, utility, topography and improvement quality can vary significantly.

Acreage and rural residential properties frequently require a broader search area because truly similar sales may be infrequent.

Property characteristics we consider

  • acreage and usable land
  • well and septic
  • topography and access
  • barns and accessory buildings
  • custom or non-standard construction
  • rural road and location influences

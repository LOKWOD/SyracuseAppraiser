LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Older housing stock and small income property require property-specific analysis.

Auburn has a substantial inventory of older housing, established neighborhoods, small residential income properties and a range of property conditions. Neighborhood, condition, functional utility and property use can be more important than straight-line distance when selecting comparable sales.

Properties with extensive renovation, conversion history or atypical condition may require a more detailed search and market-supported adjustment analysis.

Property characteristics we consider

  • neighborhood and block influence
  • older housing stock and renovation
  • single-family versus 2–4 family use
  • lot and parking utility
  • condition and deferred maintenance
  • proximity to major local amenities and corridors

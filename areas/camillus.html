LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Suburban housing can still vary materially by neighborhood and condition.

Camillus contains a broad mix of postwar housing, established subdivisions, village properties, larger suburban homes and rural-fringe properties. The most relevant comparable sales depend on the subject’s neighborhood, school district, age, style, site and level of modernization.

A property that is substantially renovated, unusually large or located on acreage may compete in a different market segment from nearby conventional homes.

Property characteristics we consider

  • school-district influences
  • subdivision versus village setting
  • split-level, ranch and colonial housing stock
  • renovation and effective age
  • lot size and utility
  • proximity to major commuter routes

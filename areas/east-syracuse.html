LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Village housing and DeWitt-address markets should not be blended automatically.

East Syracuse includes a traditional village housing stock as well as suburban properties carrying an East Syracuse postal address. Housing form, municipality, school district and proximity to commercial or industrial corridors can materially affect buyer reaction.

Older homes and small residential income properties require careful analysis of condition, legal use and the market segment most likely to purchase the property.

Property characteristics we consider

  • village versus Town of DeWitt location
  • school district
  • housing density and age
  • renovation and condition
  • small income property use
  • commercial or corridor influence

LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Established village and suburban housing need local context.

Fayetteville includes an established village core, older character homes, conventional suburban housing and nearby higher-end residential areas. Marketability can vary by street, condition, lot utility, renovation level and the competitive school-district market.

Renovated older homes can be especially sensitive to workmanship, functional layout and how buyers react to updates relative to original character.

Property characteristics we consider

  • village location and walkability
  • condition and modernization
  • architectural character
  • lot size and privacy
  • school district
  • garage, parking and site utility

LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

City housing, small income property and Lake Ontario influence require distinct analysis.

Oswego includes older city housing, small residential income properties, suburban and rural-fringe homes, and properties influenced by proximity to Lake Ontario. Market participants may react differently to condition, rental potential, water influence and neighborhood location.

Older homes, 2–4 family properties and water-influenced locations can require specialized comparable selection and careful analysis of market reaction.

Property characteristics we consider

  • city neighborhood and property use
  • Lake Ontario influence where applicable
  • older housing stock
  • small residential income characteristics
  • condition and modernization
  • site utility and parking

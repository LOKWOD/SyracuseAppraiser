COMMON QUESTIONS

Appraisal FAQ

How much does a residential appraisal cost?

Fees depend on the property, intended use, complexity, location and required scope of work. Send us the property information and intended use for a quote.

How long does an appraisal take?

Timing varies with assignment complexity, access and current scheduling. We will provide scheduling expectations when reviewing your request.

Do you work directly with homeowners and attorneys?

Yes. Private appraisal work can include estate, divorce, pre-listing, tax-related, purchase decision and other non-lending uses.

Is an appraisal the same as a real estate agent's CMA?

No. A real estate appraisal is an independent valuation assignment completed by a licensed or certified appraiser under professional appraisal standards.
